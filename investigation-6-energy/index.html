Teacher reveal: model constants and assumptions

Material constants

  • Water: 4.18 J/(g·°C)
  • Aluminum: 0.90 J/(g·°C)
  • Copper: 0.385 J/(g·°C)
  • Iron: 0.45 J/(g·°C)
  • Granite: 0.79 J/(g·°C)

Ideal-model assumptions

No energy is lost to the container or surroundings. All materials begin at 20.0 °C. The model does not represent heating rate or thermal conductivity.

Model note: This simplified investigation shows relationships among energy, mass, material, and temperature. It does not measure how quickly a sample warms.
