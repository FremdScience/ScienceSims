How to use the simulation
  1. Choose a seed, soil treatment, daily water amount, and number of lights for each pot.
  2. Select Play. You may pause at any time; the simulation stops at day 50.
  3. Open Data to compare height, mass, and appearance.
  4. Record your observations and conclusions in the separate Plants Lab handout.
