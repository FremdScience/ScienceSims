Electron Configuration Practice

Neutral atoms · main-group elements (s- and p-block) through xenon

  1. Pick an element from the table to practice, or tap Start practice set for 5 questions.
  2. Build the configuration: tap an energy level, then a subshell, then the number of electrons. Pieces appear in the order you add them. Tap a piece to move or remove it.
  3. Shortcut (optional): you may start with the noble gas right before your element, like [Ne] for chlorine. It must come first.
  4. Check: ✓ green = correct, ✗ red = wrong, ↔ amber = right subshell, wrong place. After 2 tries the answer is shown, and you enter it to move on.
  5. Finish the set: get at least 4 of 5 correct. Type your name on the results card, take a screenshot (top button + volume up), and upload it to Schoology.
